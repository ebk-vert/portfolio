Helicopter Duration Rocketry, NAR Annual Meet Championship

Silver Medalist · National Association of Rocketry, Pueblo, CO · June to August 2024

The project
A competition rocket for the Helicopter Duration event at the National Association of Rocketry's Annual Meet Championship. In this event the rocket recovers by spinning down on rotor blades instead of a parachute, and the score is how long it stays in the air.
What I did
Designed, built and flight-tested 5 custom carbon fiber prototypes over three months, changing one variable per build to isolate what drove flight duration and converge on a final configuration. The governing constraint was the motor's fixed ejection delay, so I tuned airframe mass across builds to align apogee with it.
Outcome
Silver medal: 2nd nationally in Helicopter Duration.

"Crush" Autonomous Underwater Vehicle

Lead Mechanical Designer · Harvey Mudd Robotic Submarine Team · September 2025 to present

The project
"Crush" is the Harvey Mudd Robotic Submarine Team's autonomous underwater vehicle. Its housing has to stay watertight through extended submerged operation.
What I did
  • Model the structural and housing components in SolidWorks, from concept through manufacturable part drawings.
  • Simulate stress and strain on the casing to confirm margin before fabrication.
  • Select materials and seal geometry for sustained submerged operation, weighing strength-to-weight against corrosion resistance.
  • Make design drawings and process routings, and machine parts for Crush on the lathe and mill.
Outcome
In progress since September 2025.

Wheeev, iOS app

Solo Developer · July 2026 to present

The project
An iOS app for the car and motorcycle community: find local meets, message other riders, and share real-time location.
What I did
Designed, built and shipped it alone: React Native (Expo) and TypeScript for the app, Supabase (PostgreSQL + PostGIS) for the backend, and a custom Swift module for background location. Authorization is enforced entirely in the database through Postgres row-level security rather than in the application layer.
Outcome
Live on the App Store, shipped after three App Store rejections. Roughly 514 commits, 64 database migrations, 41 database test files and 28 unit test files. Android version in progress.
