Helicopter Duration Rocketry, NAR Annual Meet Championship

Silver Medalist · National Association of Rocketry, Pueblo, CO · June to August 2024

The project
A competition rocket for the Helicopter Duration event at the National Association of Rocketry's Annual Meet Championship. In this event the rocket recovers by spinning down on rotor blades instead of a parachute, and the score is how long it stays in the air.
What I did
Designed, built and flight-tested 5 custom carbon fiber prototypes over three months, changing one variable per build to isolate what drove flight duration and converge on a final configuration. The governing constraint was the motor's fixed ejection delay, so I tuned airframe mass across builds to align apogee with it.
Outcome
Silver medal: 2nd nationally in Helicopter Duration.
